# Research Output Templates

Adapt depth to the question and include the metadata from `analysis-framework.md`.

The chat reply is a short conclusion. Formal reports also write a portable HTML board from `references/visual-report.md`. Do not replace that board with a Cursor canvas.

## Equity or event

- Question and scope
- Observed data
- Derived metrics
- Main thesis and counter-thesis
- Catalysts and risks
- Invalidation conditions
- Confidence and next observations

## Screening

- Investment hypothesis and universe
- Factor definitions and exclusions
- Candidate ranking with reasons
- Secondary verification
- False-positive risks and follow-up work

## Market or macro

- Regime assessment
- Breadth, rotation, and cross-asset evidence
- Upcoming catalysts
- Bull/base/bear scenarios
- What would change the assessment

## Portfolio

- Holdings and weights
- Exposure and concentration
- Return, volatility, drawdown, and correlation
- Stress scenarios
- Position-level and portfolio-level actions

## Options or ETF structure

- Underlying or fund identity
- Chain (`has_options`, expiries, dollar strikes, OPRA codes) or holdings (`is_etf`, AUM, NAV, truncated list)
- Live contract quote if priced (last, bid/ask, volume — no IV/Greeks)
- What the data cannot show
