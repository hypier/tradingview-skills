# Market and Macro Research Workflow

Explain the current regime from the top down and connect macro conditions to asset and sector behavior.

Combine broad market performance, breadth or leadership, sector rotation, world-economy indicators, scheduled events, and relevant news. Compare regions and asset classes over the same window where possible. Separate macro observations from market implications and identify confirming or challenging indicators.

Output regime, leading and weakening areas, catalysts, cross-asset implications, scenarios, and risks. Do not infer causality from one headline or one day’s move. Formal reports also write a `market` HTML board; see `visual-report.md`.

## MCP composition

Combine `tradingview_get_metadata`, `tradingview_get_leaderboard`, `tradingview_get_quote_batch`, `tradingview_get_world_economy_indicator_metadata`, `tradingview_get_world_economy_indicators`, `tradingview_get_calendar`, and `tradingview_get_news`. Use shared time windows when comparing markets.
