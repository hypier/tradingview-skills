# Signal Validation Workflow

Test a signal rule before treating it as a usable strategy.

Write entry, exit, holding period, universe, and sizing rules precisely. Use historical prices and relevant indicators over a stated sample, then calculate trade count, hit rate, average gain and loss, expectancy, volatility, maximum drawdown, and turnover. Include fees, spread, slippage, and delistings when data permits. Separate development and validation periods where possible.

Report parameter sensitivity, sample limitations, look-ahead or survivorship risks, and failure regimes. A historical result supports a hypothesis; it does not establish future performance.
