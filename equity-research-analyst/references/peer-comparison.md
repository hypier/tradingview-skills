# Peer Comparison

Define a comparable set by business, geography, size, and economics before ranking peers. Combine batch quotes and OHLCV with market data for growth, margins, returns, leverage, valuation, and relative performance. Explain differences rather than treating rank as a conclusion.

Use `tradingview_search_market`, `tradingview_get_quote_batch`, `tradingview_get_ohlcv_batch`, and `tradingview_get_market_data`.
