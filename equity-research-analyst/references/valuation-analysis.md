# Valuation Analysis

Build a valuation range from MCP current and historical fundamentals, enterprise value, price, dividends, and analyst expectations. Choose methods that fit the company, state assumptions, compare with peers and history, and show bull/base/bear sensitivity. Use `tradingview_get_market_data` for structured inputs and calculate multiples or simplified DCF locally. Do not present a target price without assumptions and an invalidation condition.

For detailed procedures, read `initiating-coverage/task3-valuation.md` and `initiating-coverage/valuation-methodologies.md`.
